Manoa Course Wise is a web platform we built to help UH Mānoa students make smarter choices about their classes and professors. The idea was to create a single place where students could easily search for courses, check out detailed info on professors, and read honest reviews from other students before signing up. We wanted to make the process of planning your schedule less stressful and more transparent, so everyone could benefit from the experiences of their peers.


Throughout this project, I got to work on a variety of features, from setting up the database and authentication to making sure the course and professor pages displayed real, up-to-date information. I spent a lot of time connecting the backend to the frontend, making sure that when someone searched for a course or submitted a review, everything worked smoothly and the data was accurate. I also helped troubleshoot issues with deployment and worked on making the site more user-friendly and visually appealing.


One of the biggest takeaways for me was learning how much goes on behind the scenes of a full-stack app. It’s not just about building pages that look good—there’s a lot of work involved in making sure the data is structured well, the authentication is secure, and everything runs smoothly in production. I learned how to use tools like Next.js, Prisma, and PostgreSQL, and got hands-on experience with deploying to Vercel and managing code with GitHub.


But honestly, the most valuable part of this project was working as a team. We had to constantly communicate, divide up tasks, and help each other out when we got stuck. I learned how to give and receive feedback, merge code changes without breaking things, and keep the project moving forward even when we hit roadblocks. By the end, I felt a lot more confident not just in my technical skills, but also in my ability to collaborate and contribute to a bigger goal. Manoa Course Wise was a real team effort!
